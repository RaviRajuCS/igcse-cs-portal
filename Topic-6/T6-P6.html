Characteristics and Components of Robots

This period targets the exact Cambridge 0984 requirement to describe the characteristics of a robot.

Cambridge 0984: a robot has a mechanical structure/framework, electrical components such as sensors, microprocessors and actuators, and is programmable. citeturn0search12

0–8 min · Retrieval

2 · Mechanical structure or framework

The mechanical structure is the physical body of the robot. It supports the components and allows the robot to perform its intended movements.

PartPossible function
Frame/chassisSupports and holds components.
WheelsAllow a mobile robot to move.
JointsAllow parts such as robotic arms to change position.
Arm/gripperAllows the robot to manipulate objects.
8–18 min · Teach

3 · Electrical components

ComponentRole in a robot
SensorDetects information about the robot's environment.
Microprocessor/controllerProcesses sensor data and follows programmed instructions.
ActuatorProduces movement or another physical action.
Power supplyProvides electrical energy for operation.
For the syllabus, sensors, microprocessors and actuators are specifically identified as examples of electrical components.
18–27 min · Teach

4 · Programmable

A robot is programmable: its behaviour is determined by instructions that can be stored and executed by its controller.

Example: A warehouse robot can be programmed to detect an obstacle, stop, turn and continue along a planned route.
Important: “Programmable” means that the robot's behaviour is controlled by instructions. It does not mean the robot necessarily has artificial intelligence.
27–35 min · Worked example

5 · Analyse a mobile warehouse robot

CharacteristicEvidence
Mechanical structureChassis, wheels and physical body support the robot.
Electrical componentsDistance sensors, controller and motors allow sensing, processing and movement.
ProgrammableA stored program determines how it responds to sensor readings and where it moves.
35–42 min · Comparison

6 · Robot or ordinary machine?

Electric fan: has a mechanical structure and electrical components, but by itself it is not necessarily a robot because it does not have the programmable sensing/control characteristics expected of a robot.
Mobile delivery robot: has a mechanical structure, electrical components including sensors/controller/actuators, and programmable behaviour.
42–47 min · Guided workout

7 · Describe the characteristics

A robot vacuum cleaner moves around a room, detects obstacles and follows programmed cleaning behaviour. Explain how it demonstrates the three characteristics of a robot.
Mechanical structure: physical body, wheels and moving parts. Electrical components: sensors, controller/microprocessor and motors/actuators. Programmable: stored instructions determine how it moves and responds to sensor data.
47–50 min · Exit ticket

8 · The three-part exam answer

Robot = mechanical structure + electrical components + programmable behaviour.

When asked to describe a robot's characteristics, cover all three.

4 · Worked Example — Robotic arm

A factory robot has a metal frame, joints, sensors, a microprocessor, motors and a program controlling its movements.

Mechanical structure: frame and joints. Electrical components: sensors, microprocessor and motors. Programmable: the stored program controls the movements.

5 · Workout — Explain “programmable”

Why is a robot's ability to be programmed important?
Programming allows instructions to define the robot's behaviour, including how it responds to sensor inputs and what actions its actuators perform.

6 · Interactive check

7 · Classification challenge

A programmable machine has sensors, a microprocessor, motors and a physical framework. Does it meet the three characteristics of a robot? Explain.
Yes. It has a mechanical framework, electrical components including sensors, a microprocessor and motors/actuators, and it is programmable.

8 · Exam-style question

Question: Describe three characteristics of a robot.

A robot has a mechanical structure/framework; it has electrical components such as sensors, a microprocessor and actuators; and it is programmable.

9 · Exam-style application

Question: A domestic robot vacuum moves around a room and avoids obstacles. Describe how it demonstrates the characteristics of a robot.

It has a mechanical structure such as a body and wheels. It has electrical components such as sensors, a microprocessor/controller and motors. It is programmable so that stored instructions determine how it moves and responds to sensor data.

10 · Common mistakes

  • Giving only sensors, microprocessors and actuators and forgetting the mechanical structure.
  • Forgetting that the robot must be programmable.
  • Assuming every robot must use artificial intelligence.
  • Describing an application instead of describing the characteristics.
  • Confusing the physical structure with the electrical components.

11 · Exam vocabulary

TermMeaning
