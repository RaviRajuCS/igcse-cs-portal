3 · Industry — automated production

Scenario: A factory monitors the temperature of a machine and automatically switches on cooling when the temperature becomes too high.
  1. A temperature sensor measures the machine temperature.
  2. The microprocessor compares the reading with a programmed threshold.
  3. If the threshold is exceeded, it sends a control signal.
  4. A cooling fan/motor operates to reduce the temperature.
18–27 min · Teach

4 · Transport — automatic control

Scenario: A vehicle detects an obstacle ahead and automatically applies braking.
  1. A suitable proximity/distance sensor detects the obstacle.
  2. The microprocessor processes the measured distance and programmed condition.
  3. If the obstacle is too close, a control signal is produced.
  4. An actuator operates the braking mechanism.
Strong answers explain the cause-and-effect chain, rather than simply listing “sensor, processor, actuator”.
27–35 min · Teach

6 · Weather, lighting, gaming and science

  • Weather: sensors measure conditions; the processor analyses readings and can control equipment such as a protective mechanism.
  • Lighting: a light sensor detects low light; the processor applies a rule; a lamp is switched on.
  • Gaming: sensors can detect player movement or controller input; processing determines the response; an actuator can provide vibration or movement.
  • Science: sensors collect measurements; a processor applies programmed rules; an actuator can control experimental equipment.
42–47 min · Guided workout

7 · Scenario analysis

An automatic lighting system switches lights on when the room becomes dark. Explain the roles of the sensor, microprocessor and actuator.
Sensor: light sensor detects the light level. Microprocessor: compares the reading with a programmed threshold. Actuator: lamp/light is switched on.
47–50 min · Exit ticket

8 · Three-question method

1. What physical condition/input is detected?
2. What does the microprocessor do with the data?
3. What physical action does the actuator perform?

🎯 Learning targets

  • Apply the automated-system model to unfamiliar contexts.
  • Choose a suitable sensor from the physical condition described.
  • Explain the microprocessor's programmed decision.
  • Choose a suitable actuator for the required action.
  • Write complete scenario-based answers.

1 · Quick check

A system detects soil moisture and switches on a pump when the soil is too dry. Which is the sensor?

2 · Worked Example — Factory machine

A machine stops automatically when excessive pressure is detected.
StageExplanation
SensorPressure sensor detects the pressure.
ProcessingMicroprocessor compares pressure with a safe programmed limit.
ActionAn actuator stops the machine.

3 · Workout — Transport

A vehicle detects an object that is too close and automatically activates its brakes. Identify the sensor and actuator and explain the processor's role.
Sensor: proximity/distance sensor. Processor: processes the distance data and checks the programmed condition. Actuator: braking mechanism.

4 · Interactive check

Which statement best describes the microprocessor in an automated system?

5 · Worked Example — Weather

A weather station closes a protective cover when wind speed becomes dangerously high.

Sensor: wind-speed sensor. Processor: compares wind speed with a programmed safety threshold. Actuator: motor closes the cover.

6 · Workout — Lighting

Write the complete sensor → processor → actuator explanation for an automatic street light that turns on at night.
A light sensor detects that the light level is low. The microprocessor compares the reading with the programmed threshold. It sends a control signal to the lamp, which switches on.

7 · Interactive check

A game controller detects player movement and the game responds with vibration. Which component could act as the actuator?

8 · Scenario challenge — Science

A laboratory system keeps a chemical solution at a target temperature. Explain how an automated system could achieve this.
Sensor: temperature sensor. Processor: compares the reading with the programmed target/threshold. Actuator: heater raises the temperature when required.

9 · Exam-style question

Question: An automated agricultural system waters crops when the soil becomes too dry. Describe how sensors, a microprocessor and an actuator work together.

A moisture sensor detects/measures the soil moisture. The sensor sends the data to the microprocessor. The microprocessor compares the reading with a programmed threshold. If the soil is too dry, it sends a control signal to an actuator such as a pump or valve. The actuator supplies water.

10 · Examiner technique

For scenario questions, use the verbs detects, sends, processes, compares, activates and controls. These make the sequence clear.

11 · Common mistakes

  • Choosing a sensor that does not measure the required condition.
  • Saying the sensor makes the decision.
  • Forgetting the programmed rule/threshold.
  • Choosing an actuator that cannot perform the required action.
  • Giving three component names without explaining how they interact.

12 · Self-assessment

Can you analyse an unfamiliar automated-system scenario and explain the complete process?
