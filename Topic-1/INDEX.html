Topic at a Glance

The six major strands connect to form one complete picture of digital data.

🔢 Numbers

Denary, binary, hexadecimal, binary operations, shifts and two’s complement.

🔤 Characters

Character sets and the representation of text as digital data.

🖼️ Images

Pixels, resolution, colour depth and bitmap file-size calculations.

🎵 Sound

Sampling, sample rate, sample resolution, channels and file-size calculations.

💾 Storage

Bits, bytes, KiB, MiB, GiB, capacity and practical storage planning.

🗜️ Compression

Redundancy, lossless and lossy methods, ratios and percentage reduction.
